# Calculation Verification

For important quantitative work:
1. State the requested quantity.
2. Extract known values.
3. Check units, conditions, and notation.
4. Select and justify the formula.
5. Substitute carefully.
6. Avoid premature rounding.
7. Check dimensional consistency.
8. Check magnitude and plausibility.
9. Independently verify when practical.

## Independent checks
Use a genuinely distinct check: alternative arrangement, substitution into the original equation, estimate, dimensional analysis, graph, alternate method, or permitted computational tool. Repeating the same arithmetic is not independent verification.

## Diagnose separately
Formula choice, unit conversion, substitution, arithmetic, algebra, precision, interpretation, and assumptions.

Do not claim calculator/code/simulator use unless actually used. In Socratic mode, identify the faulty step and guide the learner rather than replacing the full solution.

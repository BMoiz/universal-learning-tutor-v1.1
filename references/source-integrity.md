# Source Hierarchy, Cross-Document Mapping, and Uncertainty

## Source roles
Assign source roles based on the learner's context:
- Official syllabus: declared course scope/objectives.
- Instructor notes: instructor's explanations and notation.
- Textbook: theoretical reference.
- Tutorial/worksheet: exact exercise source.
- Past examination: historical evidence of format/content, not guaranteed future scope.
- Learner solution: diagnostic evidence, not authoritative theory.

No source is automatically authoritative for every question. Ask if authority is unclear.

## Mapping
Map chapters, sections, learning objectives, tutorials, and assessment questions. Identify prerequisite links and topic coverage. Lack of an exercise does not prove a topic is unimportant.

## Discrepancy handling
Check constants, definitions, symbols, units, rounding, missing/duplicated sections, and contradictory statements. Identify each version with its source and consequence. Do not silently correct or choose. Ask for clarification or permission for external verification when necessary.

## Content labels
Use SOURCE, EXPLANATION, DERIVATION, PREREQUISITE, EXTENSION, and EXTERNAL RESEARCH when distinction matters.

## Uncertainty labels
- CONFIRMED
- INFERRED
- ASSUMED
- UNCERTAIN
- UNKNOWN
- CONFLICTING

For important assumptions, record assumption, reason, evidence, confidence, whether confirmation is needed, and effect on the learning plan.

Exam coverage must not be inferred solely from tutorial coverage. Label provisional plans and request confirmation.
